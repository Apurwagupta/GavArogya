True Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

“Users only access the healthcare information required for their authorized role.”

GavArogya enforces zero-trust data segregation. Every stakeholder operates strictly within their clinically authorized scope, ensuring privacy, non-repudiation, and patient protection.

Role Persona What They CAN Do What They CANNOT Do (Strict RBAC Safeguards)
👤
PATIENT
Individual Citizen
  • View own health case
  • Request assistance
  • Consult GavArogya AI Assistant for primary triage
  • Contact ASHA
  • Consult doctor (eSanjeevani)
  • View referral
  • View own Care Passport
  • View follow-up status
  • CANNOT view other patients
  • CANNOT view facility-wide data
  • CANNOT view government analytics
👩‍⚕️
ASHA WORKER
Frontline Village Healthcare
  • Receive assigned patient requests
  • Record symptoms
  • Record vitals
  • Perform assessments (Rule-based AI triage)
  • View assigned patient cases
  • Complete follow-ups
  • Work offline & sync records when connectivity returns
  • CANNOT view unrelated patients outside assigned area
  • CANNOT view district-wide analytics
  • CANNOT access full facility management information
🏥
HEALTHCARE FACILITY
Hospital / PHC Operational
  • View incoming referrals
  • View authorized referral summaries
  • Scan GavArogya Care Passport
  • Accept referrals
  • Redirect referrals when capacity is unavailable
  • Update treatment status
  • Update bed and specialist availability
  • CANNOT view unrelated patient records
  • CANNOT access statewide analytics
  • CANNOT expose non-essential patient history
🏛️
GOVERNMENT / MINISTRY
Public Health Analytics
  • View anonymized and aggregated data
  • View active cases & referral completion
  • View referral delays & average referral-to-treatment time
  • Analyze district trends & heatmap
  • Track facility capability gaps & specialist shortages
  • Monitor medicine stockout risk (e-Aushadhi)
  • Track follow-up completion
  • MINISTRY MUST NOT SHOW IDENTIFIABLE PATIENT DETAILS
  • CANNOT view individual names, mobile numbers, or home addresses
  • CANNOT query raw unaggregated medical histories
